Covers "not in breach of UK immigration laws" and the invitation checkbox.

Do not send these

The guide says these are not used as evidence.

  • Bank statements older than 1 year
  • Credit card statements
  • Driving licenses
  • Educational certificates
  • Car ownership papers
  • Personal photographs
  • Notarial certificates
  • Business cards
  • Hotel bookings
  • Flight bookings
  • Photocopies of bank cards
  • Travel insurance
  • Sponsor's utility bills
  • Sponsor's council tax bills
Based on the GOV.UK guide to supporting documents for visiting the UK. The guide's list is not exhaustive and sending these documents does not guarantee a visa. This is a personal checklist, not legal advice. Ticks are saved in this browser only.
